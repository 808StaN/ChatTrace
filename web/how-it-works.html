INSIDE THE EXTENSION

From live chat to full context in seconds.

ChatTrace runs directly inside Twitch so you never lose your place on stream. Click any chatter to open their user card or type /logs [username] in chat to browse, search, and read past messages right where the conversation happens.

  • User card & /logs command
  • 7TV, BTTV & FFZ emotes
  • English & Polish UI
  • No registration
TWO WAYS TO OPEN

Open history however you use chat

Whether someone just sent a message in live chat or you already know their username, ChatTrace opens their channel history without leaving the stream.

WAY 01 · CLICK IN CHAT

From any Twitch user card

Click a chatter's username in Twitch chat (or your own) to open their normal viewer card, then click Chat history. The history panel docks right beside the card, moves with it when dragged, and closes automatically when you dismiss the card.

WAY 02 · SLASH COMMAND

With the /logs [username] command

Don't want to scroll back looking for a message? Type /logs username (or /logs @username) straight into the Twitch chat input. ChatTrace registers directly in Twitch's native command menu and opens a standalone, draggable history window.

INSIDE THE PANEL

Built to feel native on Twitch

Every detail of the history panel is designed to match Twitch's dark interface and keep fast-moving conversations readable.

Instant message search

Filter loaded messages in real time as you type to quickly jump to a specific topic, question, or reaction in the current channel.

Grouped by date & paginated

Messages are sorted newest-first under clear day headers with timestamps, loaded in fast 50-message pages with one-click Load older messages (up to 2,000 messages per view).

Badges & 3rd-party emotes

Renders public Twitch channel badges alongside 7TV, BetterTTV, and FrankerFaceZ emotes so past messages read just like live Twitch chat.

Draggable & bilingual

Grab the panel header to reposition it anywhere in the viewport. Labels automatically follow your Twitch language in English and Polish.

UNDER THE HOOD · PRIVACY FIRST

Direct from your browser. Zero ChatTrace servers.

ChatTrace does not run a bot, crawler, analytics tracker, or backend database. Requests happen directly from your browser only when you open a chatter's history.

  1. 1
    Read channel & chatter

    Detects the currently watched Twitch channel login and the username you clicked or passed to /logs.

  2. 2
    Fetch public logs & emotes

    Queries logs.zonian.dev for available public chat logs, plus api.ivr.fi, 7tv.io, api.betterttv.net, and api.frankerfacez.com for badges and emotes.

  3. 3
    Keep only in tab memory

    Loaded messages stay in a short-lived in-memory cache while the tab is open and are cleared as soon as you close or reload the page.

Good to know

  • Chat history is available on channels covered by the public logs.zonian.dev infrastructure.
  • Private whispers, unrecorded messages, or deleted messages are never collected and cannot be recovered.
  • No remote code is downloaded or executed — all extension logic ships inside the verified Chrome Web Store package. Read our Privacy policy for full details.
